std.map

Hash maps.

Types

warp Map[K, V] builtin

A map from K to V that remembers insertion order: going through it, and showing it, follow the order keys were first added. Build one with Map(), or a literal where a map is expected: let ages: Map[str, u8] = ["ada": 36, "alan": 41] ([:] is empty). m[k] is the value for k, and unravels when there's none (m.get(k) gives an optional); m[k] = v adds or replaces it. for (k, v) in m goes through the pairs, and a map has the methods of Stream too, over its pairs. Keys must be Hash.
